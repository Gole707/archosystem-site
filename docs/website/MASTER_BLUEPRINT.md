# ArchoSystem Website and Investor Experience — Master Blueprint

**Status:** Planning baseline v1.0  
**Owner:** ArchoSystem  
**Scope:** Public website, private investor experience, ArchoTank selector, disclosure controls, visual system, and build governance

---

## 1. Executive decision

The current website is visually strong and technically serviceable, but its product story reflects an earlier version of ArchoSystem: controlled field formation, POD execution units, ArchoOS lifecycle control, and a primarily hardened UHPC ArchoTank output.

The next website must reflect the current architecture:

> ArchoSystem is a configurable infrastructure platform. ArchoTank is its first containment product family, configured by capacity, geometry, stored contents, climate, service duration, protection level, accessories, and lifecycle control.

The rebuild will preserve the existing premium industrial visual identity and lightweight static codebase, while replacing the information architecture and product narrative.

The work will produce three connected experiences:

1. **Public ArchoSystem website** — clear, credible, disciplined product communication.
2. **Private investor experience** — market scale, capture logic, recurring revenue, economics, defensibility, and platform expansion.
3. **ArchoTank Selector V1** — a transparent configuration and capacity tool that turns project constraints into a preliminary system recommendation.

No live-site code is changed until the strategy, copy, claims, visual system, and wireframes pass their approval gates.

## 2. End state

### 2.1 Public website

The public site must allow a first-time visitor to understand within one short visit:

- what ArchoSystem is;
- what ArchoTank does;
- how transported size is separated from deployed capacity;
- how the system changes by contents, climate, duration, and protection;
- how temporary, reusable, permanent, and hardened configurations relate;
- what is being calculated and tested now;
- how to begin a technical, pilot, procurement, or investment conversation.

The public site should create confidence without publishing the complete market strategy, detailed internal construction recipe, confidential economics, or every future product extension.

### 2.2 Private investor experience

The investor experience must show:

- ArchoTank as a market-entry product and installed-base engine;
- the full sector opportunity map;
- market-by-market TAM, SAM, and SOM logic;
- unit-based capture scenarios rather than arbitrary market-share percentages;
- revenue from systems, staged readiness inventory, replacement materials, accessories, aesthetic packages, software, service, and refurbishment;
- competitive position by job and use case;
- validation and certification roadmap;
- capital required and the technical or commercial risk removed by each use of funds;
- future ArchoSystem platform expansion.

The private experience must be authenticated or separately deployed. An unlinked public URL is not adequate protection.

### 2.3 ArchoTank Selector V1

The selector must calculate and explain, not overpromise. It will accept capacity and site constraints, calculate feasible geometries, show nominal and working capacity, estimate hydrostatic demand, and recommend a preliminary configuration class.

It is not a final engineering design, quote generator, code-compliance tool, or AI advisor in Version 1.

## 3. Current product narrative

### 3.1 ArchoSystem

ArchoSystem is the parent platform that coordinates configurable physical infrastructure, deployment, validation, accessories, readiness, monitoring, and lifecycle records.

### 3.2 ArchoTank

ArchoTank is the first product family: a configurable structural containment system capable of serving rapid, reusable, semi-permanent, permanent, and hardened storage applications.

The product is configured across independent decision axes:

| Decision axis | Customer-facing purpose |
|---|---|
| Capacity and geometry | Match volume, footprint, height, and site access |
| Inner containment barrier | Match stored contents, sanitation, temperature, and chemical compatibility |
| Structural or activation medium | Match deployment speed, recoverability, mass, stiffness, permanence, and protection |
| Climate package | Match freezing, heat, biological stability, thermal expansion, and environmental exposure |
| Exterior protection | Match UV, abrasion, vandalism, puncture, fragments, fire exposure, camouflage, and architectural needs |
| Base and anchoring | Match soil, wind, uplift, flooding, settlement, and deployment duration |
| Accessories | Match access, venting, piping, overflow, sampling, instrumentation, safety, and operations |
| Monitoring and records | Match readiness, inspection, telemetry, warranty, deployment history, and compliance evidence |

The public story explains these functions without turning a specific commercial material construction method into the product identity.

### 3.3 ArchoOS

ArchoOS is the control and evidence layer around each configured asset. It records configuration, material and component identity, deployment state, inspections, sensors, repairs, replacement cycles, readiness, and lifecycle history.

ArchoOS supports the physical product story; it should not block a visitor from first understanding ArchoTank.

## 7. Market sizing and capture logic

Investor pages must not take an enormous sector total and apply an unsupported percentage.

For each sector:

1. Define the total sector.
2. Isolate the relevant storage or containment portion.
3. Remove applications outside the technical configuration envelope.
4. Identify reachable geographies and buyer classes.
5. Estimate units per buyer and likely buying cycle.
6. Apply representative system, accessories, consumables, software, and service revenue.
7. Show conservative, base, and expansion scenarios.
8. State assumptions, confidence, barriers, and evidence.

A preferred SOM formula is:

> Reachable buyers × systems per buyer × average initial revenue + annual installed-base revenue.

This produces a more defensible capture model than an arbitrary percentage of TAM.

## 11. Build governance

### 11.1 Three approval gates

1. **Strategy and content gate** — sitemap, copy, terminology, claims, disclosure, selector rules.
2. **Visual and wireframe gate** — canonical product, image masters, page flow, responsive ordering, design system.
3. **Working-site gate** — implementation, calculations, access control, QA, production readiness.

### 11.2 Revision limit

Each gate receives no more than two planned revision rounds:

- Revision A: substantive correction.
- Revision B: final polish.

A closed gate reopens only for a factual, legal, technical, accessibility, security, or material usability issue.

### 11.3 Change control

- Settled decisions are recorded in `DECISIONS.md`.
- Claims are controlled in `CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`.
- New launch ideas enter a later-version parking lot.
- Page-specific styling cannot override the design system without a documented functional reason.
- The founder approves direction, disclosure, claims, and launch; the founder does not become the CSS operator.

## 14. Definition of done

The rebuild is complete when:

- approved public pages are live;
- the current product narrative is accurate;
- public and private disclosure boundaries are enforced;
- the investor portal uses real authentication or separate controlled deployment;
- market cards use documented sources and assumptions;
- Selector V1 calculations pass independent checks;
- cinematic imagery follows one canonical product design;
- every public claim is present in the claims ledger;
- production inquiry paths work;
- keyboard, mobile, tablet, and desktop QA passes at the agreed target widths;
- critical and major issues are resolved;
- decision, claims, terminology, visual, selector, and workflow documents are current;
- the site is frozen after launch except for genuine defects and evidence-driven changes.

The build is not incomplete merely because another animation, market, image variation, small spacing adjustment, or future feature could be added.

## 15. Source inputs and validation requirements

Planning and copy will draw from:

- the current ArchoSystem website and repository;
- ArchoTank installation and QC documents;
- patent materials and counsel-approved terminology;
- commercially available structural-material and fabrication data;
- validated market sources;
- capacity and hydrostatic calculations;
- prototype and test results as they become available;

Existing internal market reports are working inputs, not automatically publishable evidence. Every load-bearing market or performance claim must be revalidated before publication.
