# ArchoSystem Website Rebuild — Claims Ledger

Every performance, capacity, market, operating, and business claim used in public or private materials must appear here before publication.

**Status definitions**

- **Verified** — supported by current authoritative evidence.
- **Calculated** — derived from transparent mathematics and stated assumptions.
- **Supported concept** — technically plausible with precedent, but ArchoSystem configuration is not yet validated.
- **Planned** — a development or commercial objective, not a present capability.
- **Unverified** — insufficient evidence; do not claim.

## Publication workflow

Before copy is approved:

1. Add or update the claim here.
2. Identify the evidence and date.
3. Select the exact allowed wording.
4. Confirm whether the wording is public, private, NDA-only, or engineering-only.
5. Record any conditions, exclusions, or expiration date.
6. Recheck the claim during final QA.
