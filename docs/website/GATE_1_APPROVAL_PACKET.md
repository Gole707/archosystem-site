## 2. Final public product hierarchy

### ArchoSystem

ArchoSystem is the parent deployable-infrastructure platform. It coordinates configured physical systems, deployment, validation, accessories, readiness, monitoring, service, and lifecycle records.

### ArchoTank

ArchoTank is the first product family: a configurable structural containment system designed around deployed capacity, stored contents, climate, service duration, exterior exposure, site conditions, accessories, and lifecycle validation.

### ArchoOS

ArchoOS is the planned control and evidence layer around each configured asset. It supports configuration, identification, inspections, monitoring, repairs, replacement cycles, readiness, deployment history, and lifecycle evidence.

**Public ordering is locked:** ArchoTank first, ArchoOS after the physical product is understood.

## 3. Public positioning

### Approved homepage hero

**Eyebrow:** Deployable containment infrastructure

# Infrastructure-scale containment, designed to travel compact.

**Lead:** ArchoTank is a configurable structural containment system designed around capacity, contents, climate, service duration, protection, and lifecycle validation.

**Primary CTA:** Explore ArchoTank  
**Secondary CTA:** Discuss a requirement

### Core public explanation

ArchoTank separates the transported package from the deployed asset. The system is configured around the project rather than forcing the project into one fixed tank material, size, service class, or exterior package.

The public site explains six customer-facing decisions:

1. capacity and geometry;
2. stored-content compatibility;
3. deployment and service class;
4. climate and environmental exposure;
5. exterior protection and identity;
6. accessories, monitoring, and lifecycle support.

### Public proof posture

The site does not present development goals as completed capabilities. It shows:

- what is established by geometry and transparent calculation;
- what is supported by commercial material precedent;
- what is under development;
- what requires prototype or third-party validation;
- what is intentionally not claimed.

## 6. Private investor experience

The investor experience reveals the scale intentionally withheld from the public site.

### Private navigation

`Investment Overview · Opportunity Atlas · Sector Explorer · Product Map · Revenue Engine · Economics · Competition · Validation · IP · Capital Plan · Data Room`

### Core investor narrative

ArchoTank is not only a tank sale. It is an installed-base and readiness platform.

The commercial flywheel is:

> System sale → installed asset → accessories → ArchoOS → readiness contract → deployment → consumables and replacement use → refurbishment → redeployment → fleet expansion.

### Opportunity Atlas navigation

> World → sector → geography → buyer class → asset need → ArchoTank configuration → revenue opportunity → evidence.

The atlas is a working demonstration of the broader Archo Intelligence navigation model, not a decorative map.

### Market-capture rule

Market sizing will not use arbitrary percentages of giant sector totals.

Each sector must show:

1. total sector definition;
2. relevant storage or containment portion;
3. technically suitable portion;
4. reachable geography and buyer class;
5. systems per buyer and buying cycle;
6. initial system and accessory revenue;
7. installed-base annual revenue;
8. conservative, base, and expansion scenarios;
9. assumptions, confidence, barriers, and evidence.

Preferred SOM expression:

> Reachable buyers × systems per buyer × average initial revenue + annual installed-base revenue.

## 9. Terminology and claims approval

### Public identity

Use:

- configurable structural containment system;
- deployable infrastructure platform;
- structural wall system;
- content-specific inner barrier;
- engineered activation or structural medium;
- replaceable exterior protection;
- climate-specific formulation;
- accessory and process-interface package;
- monitoring, readiness, and lifecycle evidence.

Do not lead public marketing with the name of one commercial textile construction method.

### Current claim posture

Allowed:

- designed to separate transported package size from deployed capacity;
- nominal capacities derived from stated geometry;
- engineered configurations may exceed one million gallons;
- configured for project requirements;
- designed for rapid deployment, reduced field labor, recovery, and redeployment;
- full-height, pneumatic, engineered-liquid, cementitious, and hardened configurations are under development;
- inner barriers are selected for the stored contents;
- replaceable exterior protection can be configured by exposure.

Not allowed until supported:

- stores anything;
- any climate or any scale as an absolute claim;
- two-person installation as a completed capability;
- exact setup time or savings percentage;
- bulletproof, knife-proof, puncture-proof, fragment-proof, or blast-proof;
- zero corrosion, zero maintenance, or exact service life;
- agency approval, certification, selection, or use without evidence.

## 10. Selector V1 boundary

Selector V1 is approved as a transparent calculator and rules engine.

It may calculate:

- nominal cylindrical volume;
- working capacity after an explicitly stated freeboard assumption;
- diameter required for a target volume and height;
- height required for a target volume and diameter;
- base hydrostatic pressure;
- alternate low-and-wide and compact-footprint geometries;
- preliminary service, barrier, climate, and protection categories.

It is not a final engineering design, quote, code review, certification tool, or AI recommendation engine.
