How the system is delivered

The tank does not travel at its deployed size.

ArchoTank moves as a compact manufactured package. The final geometry, operating equipment, and selected structural configuration are established at the project site.

01

Packed

Tank body, containment barrier, selected exterior system, accessories, deployment equipment, and documentation.

02

Positioned

The system is placed, oriented, connected to the required foundation or anchoring package, and established to its design geometry.

03

Activated

The selected pneumatic, stabilized, cementitious, or combined configuration is commissioned through the project deployment process.

04

In service

Connections, access, monitoring, inspection, replacement parts, and lifecycle records support the operating asset.

Complete operating packages

The tank is supplied as a system—not a bare shell.

Packages organize the components engineers, contractors, operators, and deployment teams need to install, connect, operate, inspect, repair, and support the asset.

ArchoDeploy

Blowers, compressors, manifolds, pumps, hoses, anchors, slab attachments, tools, repair equipment, and transport packaging.

ArchoFlow

Inlets, outlets, drains, overflows, vents, relief devices, sampling, mixing, recirculation, and instrument connections.

ArchoAccess

Ladders, stairs, platforms, handrails, walkways, roof access, inspection hatches, and equipment supports.

ArchoProtect

UV, abrasion, thermal, weather, lower-wall, fitting, vandal, and mission-specific exterior protection.

ArchoSkin

Customer colors, municipal identity, safety markings, high visibility, low visibility, architectural finish, and custom graphics.

ArchoSense

Level, pressure, temperature, leak, deformation, inspection, readiness, and asset-identification hardware.

ArchoReady

Staged replacement stock, annual inspection, deployment exercises, repair inventory, refurbishment, and recertification.

ArchoOS

Planned configuration, deployment, inspection, monitoring, repair, replacement, and lifecycle evidence layer.
