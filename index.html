Intended delivery sequence

Separate transported package size from deployed capacity.

ArchoTank is being designed to move as a compact manufactured package, with final geometry, operating equipment, and structural configuration established through a project-specific site process.

01

Packed

Planned project package: structural body, containment barrier, selected exterior system, accessories, deployment equipment, and documentation.

02

Positioned

The planned process places and orients the system at the prepared foundation and restraint interface.

03

Configured

Project-specific geometry, interfaces, access, protection, and monitoring are established under the deployment plan.

04

Commissioned

Testing, inspection, documentation, and applicable approvals determine readiness for the intended service.

Tank families

One platform. Purpose-specific systems.

The common structural platform is configured into tank families with different operating packages, accessories, engineering criteria, and validation pathways.

Rapid pathway

ArchoTank Rapid

A development configuration for temporary storage where compact transport, deployment planning, recovery, and staged readiness matter.

  • Emergency and utility support
  • Remote and difficult-access sites
  • Seasonal or temporary capacity
General storage

ArchoTank Storage

Project-specific liquid storage configured by capacity, content barrier, climate, foundation, access, and monitoring.

  • Water and wastewater
  • Agricultural and industrial liquids
  • Expansion and rehabilitation sequencing
Fire-water pathway

ArchoTank Fire

A development configuration organized around reserve capacity, flow interfaces, access, monitoring, and site conditions.

  • Industrial and commercial requirements
  • Remote sites and temporary coverage
  • Project engineering and authority approval required
Process capacity

ArchoTank Process

Containment packages for equalization, bypass, mixing, process holding, temporary treatment, and rehabilitation support.

  • Municipal and industrial wastewater
  • Temporary process capacity
  • Partner-integrated process equipment
Long-duration pathway

ArchoTank Permanent

A development configuration for project-specific foundations, permanent process connections, durable exterior systems, and long-duration structural pathways.

  • Municipal and industrial requirements
  • Infrastructure-scale geometry studies
  • Project engineering and validation required
Future test program

ArchoTank Hardened

A research pathway for defined protective requirements, subject to system-level engineering and testing.

  • High-consequence infrastructure research
  • Protected-interface development
  • No ballistic or blast rating claimed

Development package architecture

The product is being developed as a system—not a bare shell.

Package categories organize what engineers, contractors, operators, and deployment teams may need to install, connect, operate, inspect, repair, and support a future validated asset.

ArchoDeploy

Blowers, compressors, manifolds, pumps, hoses, anchors, slab attachments, tools, repair equipment, and transport packaging.

ArchoFlow

Inlets, outlets, drains, overflows, vents, relief devices, sampling, mixing, recirculation, and instrument connections.

ArchoAccess

Ladders, stairs, platforms, handrails, walkways, roof access, inspection hatches, and equipment supports.

ArchoProtect

Development categories for UV, abrasion, thermal, weather, lower-wall, fitting, and site-specific exterior protection.

ArchoSkin

Customer colors, municipal identity, safety markings, high visibility, low visibility, architectural finish, and custom graphics.

ArchoSense

Level, pressure, temperature, leak, deformation, inspection, readiness, and asset-identification hardware.

ArchoReady

Staged replacement stock, annual inspection, deployment exercises, repair inventory, refurbishment, and recertification.

ArchoOS

Planned configuration, deployment, inspection, monitoring, repair, replacement, and lifecycle evidence layer.
