The Problem

Infrastructure delivery is still too slow, fragmented, and hard to verify.

Field labor, formwork, equipment and time on site constrain how much infrastructure a team can deliver. ArchoTank is designed to reduce that installation burden through a different formation and deployment method.

See the Shift
01

Labor & Site Time

People × hours × days determine field labor-hours per tank. Reducing that requirement can release crews for the next project.

Hard to Verify

The Shift

From Construction to Controlled Deployment

The intended delivery model carries each asset through a defined execution sequence. Prototype testing will establish the achievable labor, schedule and performance results.

First Output: ArchoTank

ArchoTank is our patent-pending deployable infrastructure system for storage and containment. It is designed to reduce conventional formwork, field labor-hours, equipment dependence and time on site.

Designed for water, wastewater, emergency storage, fuel-support, industrial containment, and future infrastructure applications, subject to project-specific engineering.
