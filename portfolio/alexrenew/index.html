Alexandria, VA

AlexRenew Nutrient Management Facility

Wastewater storage and public recreation

AlexRenew built an 18-million-gallon wastewater storage tank beneath a public athletic field. The project added room to manage treatment flows while giving Alexandria a place to play soccer and exercise. [2] [1]

Why it happened

The Nutrient Management Facility was part of AlexRenew’s program to reduce nitrogen discharged into the Potomac River and Chesapeake Bay. Storage helps balance the flows going through treatment. The site also offered a chance to build a public field directly above the tank. [2] [1] [3]

What was built

The contract connected the tank to the treatment plant and included a pump station, odor control, and electrical infrastructure. The tank package changed from cast-in-place construction to a precast, post-tensioned structure. Its roof supports Limerick Field. [3] [5] [1]

What changed

The two uses now share one footprint. The treatment plant has a large storage buffer below grade, and the public has an athletic field above it. The population figure describes the treatment facility served by the storage system. [1] [4]
