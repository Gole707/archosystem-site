Stroudsburg, PA

Brodhead Creek Regional Wastewater Treatment Plant

Regional wastewater treatment and service expansion

Stroudsburg’s borough plant became a regional treatment facility. The expansion increased treatment capacity while neighboring communities arranged to share service and protect the receiving streams. [1] [3] [4]

Why it happened

Stroudsburg and neighboring municipalities needed treatment capacity for a shared service area. A regional agreement provided for transfer of the former borough plant to the Brodhead Creek Regional Authority, which took ownership and began operating it in March 2011. [2] [1]

What was built

The Delaware River Basin Commission approved expansion from 2.5 to 4.5 million gallons per day and conversion to sequencing batch treatment. The current facility treats wastewater in batches and uses ultraviolet disinfection before discharge to McMichael Creek. [3] [4]

What changed

The facility treats wastewater from Stroudsburg and portions of Stroud, Pocono, Paradise, Hamilton, and Tobyhanna townships. Its discharge is regulated within the Delaware River Basin’s Special Protection Waters framework. [4]
