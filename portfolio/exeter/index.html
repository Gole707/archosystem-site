Exeter, NH

Exeter Wastewater Treatment Facility Upgrade

Nutrient removal and wastewater conveyance

Exeter replaced an aerated lagoon process with a plant designed for biological nitrogen removal. The upgrade also rebuilt critical conveyance infrastructure serving the Squamscott River and Great Bay watershed. [1] [2]

Why it happened

Exeter needed to address aging treatment infrastructure and reduce nitrogen entering the Squamscott River and Great Bay. The town serves wastewater customers in Exeter and portions of Stratham and Hampton. [2] [5]

What was built

The plant uses a four-stage Bardenpho process for biological nitrogen removal. The program also upgraded the main pump station, installed two force mains, and added a septage receiving station. [1] [2]

What changed

EPA reports that nitrogen levels fell within a month of startup in June 2019. The agency also credits the continued use of the existing lagoons and the main pump station upgrade with eliminating most of the town’s combined sewer overflows. [2]
