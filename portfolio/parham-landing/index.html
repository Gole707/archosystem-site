New Kent County, VA

Parham Landing Wastewater Treatment Plant Expansion

Wastewater expansion and water reuse

New Kent County expanded its Parham Landing plant and built a way to put treated wastewater back to use. The project increased treatment capacity and supported bulk irrigation with reclaimed water. [3] [2]

Why it happened

New Kent chose to consolidate treatment at Parham Landing and close its Chickahominy plant. Expanding Parham Landing also supported the county’s plan to use reclaimed water in place of groundwater for irrigation. [3]

What was built

The project expanded treatment and added equalization capacity. EPA documents a 10.5-mile reclaimed-water force main built to carry treated effluent to reuse customers. [3] [2]

What changed

Reclaimed water can be used for irrigation and dust control, replacing some demand for potable groundwater and reducing the amount of treated effluent discharged to the Pamunkey River. [3] [1]
