Moorefield–Hardy County Regional Wastewater Plant

Municipal and industrial wastewater treatment

Moorefield and Hardy County built one regional plant to treat municipal sewage and wastewater from poultry processing. The shared facility was designed around tighter nutrient limits in the Chesapeake Bay watershed. [1] [2]

Why it happened

The project brought municipal sewage and industrial wastewater to the same treatment plant. EPA reports that the regional facility replaced four aging plants, including two operated by the local poultry processor, Pilgrim’s Pride. [1] [2]

What was built

The regional plant includes headworks, an oxidation ditch, clarifiers, and solids handling. Separate work installed the pump station and force main that connect flows to the new treatment site. [3] [1]

What changed

The plant opened in November 2013 and discharges to the South Branch of the Potomac River. In 2015, EPA reported that early monitoring showed nitrogen and phosphorus discharges well below permit limits. The 2022 residential population captures only the residential portion of the service base. [1] [4]
