Dairy Renewable Natural Gas Projects

On-farm anaerobic digestion and gas upgrading

Oakridge, Marks, Lincoln, and Curtin are four projects built to turn dairy manure into renewable natural gas. Oakridge Dairy in Ellington, Connecticut, was the first farm in the SJI and REV LNG program to break ground. [1] [2]

Oakridge Dairy

Oakridge added a covered digester and gas-upgrading equipment to its manure-handling system. EnviTec reports that the upgraded gas is transported by trailer to a pipeline injection point. [3] [4]

How it fits the farm

Connecticut’s draft air permit describes two paths out of the digester. Biogas goes to the upgrading equipment, while the remaining material goes back to the farm for separation. The farm can reuse the solids as bedding and send the liquid to long-term storage. [3]
