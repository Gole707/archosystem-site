World population · Estimated

A changing estimate of the people sharing this planet.

Archo interpolates between two United Nations World Population Prospects 2024 medium-variant projections:

  • 1 July 2026: 8,300,678,395 people.
  • 1 July 2027: 8,369,094,344 people.

The display spreads that projected change evenly across elapsed UTC time between those dates. It uses your device clock and updates once per second. It is a modeled illustration, not a live census or a count of observed births and deaths. Its individual digits do not represent measurement accuracy.

Reference date: 1 July 2026.
