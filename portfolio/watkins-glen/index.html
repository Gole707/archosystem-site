Schuyler County, NY

Catharine Valley Water Reclamation Facility

Regional wastewater treatment · Project Seneca

Watkins Glen and Montour Falls built a shared treatment system to replace two aging plants, meet more stringent discharge requirements, and make the old Watkins Glen waterfront site available for redevelopment. [1] [5]

Why it happened

Watkins Glen faced a consent order to improve its plant. Montour Falls also needed a major upgrade. The two villages made a shared facility the first infrastructure project under Project Seneca, tying wastewater improvements to plans for the south shore of Seneca Lake. [1] [5]

What was built

The project included a regional plant, two pump stations, and about 15,360 feet of new force main specified to bring wastewater to the facility. The 4.2-million-gallon precast sequencing batch reactor treats wastewater in batches. The 1.2-million-gallon-per-day figure describes the entire plant’s design flow. [2] [3] [8]

What changed

The regional facility serves Watkins Glen, Montour Falls, and part of the Town of Dix. The consolidation allowed both older plants to be retired. In Watkins Glen, that made the former Seneca Lake site available for redevelopment and potential public waterfront access. [6] [5]
