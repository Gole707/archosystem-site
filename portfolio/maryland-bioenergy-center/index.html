Jessup, MD

Maryland Bioenergy Center

Food waste recycling and renewable natural gas

The Maryland Bioenergy Center was built beside a major food distribution hub. Its digesters turn food-processing and distribution waste into renewable gas and leave a material that can be used to make soil products. [1] [2]

Why it happened

The Maryland Food Center brings wholesalers, processors, and distribution businesses together. Locating digestion next to the campus gives area businesses a way to recycle organic material that would otherwise have to be managed off site. [1] [4]

What was built

Food waste enters sealed tanks where microorganisms break it down without oxygen. The process produces biogas that can be upgraded to renewable natural gas. The remaining digestate can be used to make soil products. [1] [2] [3]

What changed

The project created a regional destination for food waste and a source of renewable gas. The annual figure is the processing estimate in Maryland Environmental Service’s report. It describes expected throughput. [4] [1]
