Lynchburg, VA

Lynchburg Wet-Weather Storage and Overflow Reduction

Combined sewer overflow reduction

Lynchburg added wet-weather storage and expanded its ability to pump and treat storm flows. The project was part of the city’s plan to reduce combined sewer overflows into the James River. [1] [3]

Why it happened

In parts of Lynchburg, wastewater and stormwater share the same sewer. Heavy rain can bring more water into those pipes than the system can carry or treat at once. Storage gives the plant a buffer for peak flows. [1] [3]

What was built

The 2021 improvement project added a four-million-gallon precast, post-tensioned tank, upgraded screening and grit removal, increased pumping capacity, and converted a clarifier for high-strength wastewater storage. [3] [5]

What changed

The plant gained more capacity to hold, pump, screen, and disinfect flows during storms. This work was one stage in Lynchburg’s long-term overflow control program. The city’s later Blackwater tunnel is a separate project. [3] [2]
